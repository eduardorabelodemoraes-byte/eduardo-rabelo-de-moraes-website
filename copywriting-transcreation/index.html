Copywriting & Transcreation

Some messages need to be translated. Others need to be recreated. And some should be written for the Brazilian market from the start.

I write original Brazilian Portuguese copy and adapt English-language marketing for Brazilian readers, with attention to what the message needs to do and how the brand should sound.

Three ways a message reaches a market

Translate

When the wording carries information people need to rely on, the priority is to preserve meaning, terminology and function in Brazilian Portuguese. Product instructions and specifications often call for this approach.

Transcreate

When a headline depends on wordplay or a cultural reference, keeping the same effect may mean changing the line. The source brief and the brand’s voice guide what changes.

Write for the market

When the brief starts with a Brazilian audience, I develop the message directly in Portuguese. The product, the channel and the reader’s situation shape the writing from the first draft.

Services

Original Brazilian Portuguese Copywriting

I write launch emails, product pages and promotional copy from a brief. For a product page, that might mean putting the benefit readers care about first and giving them the specification that explains it.

Marketing Transcreation and Cultural Adaptation

I adapt English-language headlines, campaign messages and calls to action for Brazil. I work within the brand’s guidelines and the space available, adjusting register and cultural references where they affect how the message lands.

Brand Voice and Editorial Content

I adapt key messages and tone for Brazilian readers and use that voice consistently across formats. That can include a researched article or newsletter when the subject calls for more room than campaign copy allows.

From brief to final copy

  1. Objective and audience · Agree on who the copy is for and what they should understand or do next.
  2. Product and context · Review the product, the evidence for its claims and the constraints of the channel.
  3. Voice and message · Agree on what the brand needs to convey and how it should sound in Brazil.
  4. Writing or transcreation · Draft in Brazilian Portuguese and flag decisions that affect meaning, tone or emphasis.
  5. Review and refinement · Refine the copy with your feedback and check it against the brief, the voice and its intended use.
