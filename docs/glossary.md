# glossary.md

# THE THRESHOLD GLOSSARY

## Official Vocabulary

Version 2.0

---

# Purpose

The Glossary establishes the official meaning of the project's terminology.

Every document in the repository shall use these definitions consistently.

Whenever ambiguity arises, the definitions contained here prevail.

---

# Architecture

The permanent conceptual organization of the project.

Architecture exists independently of implementation.

Technology may evolve.

Architecture remains.

---

# Canon

The collection of permanent project decisions.

The Canon contains only explicitly approved decisions.

It never records hypotheses.

It never records experiments.

It never records temporary implementations.

---

# Collaboration

Any contribution made by a human or artificial collaborator to the project.

Every collaboration begins with reading the institutional documentation.

---

# Crossing

The consciously experienced passage between two Editorial Worlds.

Crossing is the architectural center of the project.

Crossing is **not**:

- navigation;
- animation;
- transition;
- loading;
- visual effect.

Crossing is a perceptual experience.

---

# Current Mission

The single active objective currently pursued by the project.

Only one Current Mission exists at any given moment.

---

# Documentation

The permanent body of institutional knowledge governing the project.

Documentation possesses authority over conversations.

---

# Editorial Integrity

The preservation of typography, hierarchy, spacing, rhythm, proportion and legibility throughout every experience.

Editorial Integrity is never sacrificed.

---

# Editorial World

A complete perceptual environment representing one coherent professional domain.

An Editorial World possesses:

its own atmosphere;

its own rhythm;

its own spatial logic;

its own perceptual identity.

Current Editorial Worlds:

Home

Game Localization

---

# Experiment

A controlled investigation designed to answer one clearly defined question.

Experiments generate knowledge.

They do not generate permanent features.

---

# Handbook

The institutional document defining operational methodology.

---

# Home

The primary Editorial World.

The Home establishes:

trust;

precision;

editorial restraint;

professional judgment.

---

# Hypothesis

A perceptual prediction capable of being confirmed or refuted through observation.

Hypotheses never describe implementation.

They describe expected perception.

---

# Identity

The highest conceptual layer of the project.

Identity defines purpose.

Identity defines values.

Identity precedes architecture.

Architecture precedes implementation.

---

# Implementation

The technological realization of approved perceptual objectives.

Implementation is temporary.

Implementation possesses no institutional authority.

---

# Inference

A conclusion derived from observation.

Inference is never presented as observation.

---

# Laboratory

The research environment responsible for reducing uncertainty through controlled experimentation.

---

# Laboratory Log

The permanent chronological record of every experiment performed by the project.

Entries are never rewritten.

---

# Manifesto

The institutional document defining the philosophical identity of Threshold.

---

# Material Condition

The apparent perceptual substance of an Editorial World.

Material Condition may evolve.

Identity does not.

Recognition does not.

Editorial Integrity does not.

---

# Observation

A direct report of what an observer perceived.

Observation always precedes interpretation.

---

# Observer

A person reporting spontaneous perception without prior explanation.

---

# Perception

The direct conscious experience of an observer.

Perception is the primary object of investigation within the Threshold Laboratory.

---

# Phenomenology

The description and study of perception as experienced by the observer.

Phenomenology always precedes implementation.

---

# Project

The complete institutional system composed of:

Identity;

Architecture;

Documentation;

Editorial Worlds;

Crossings;

Implementation.

---

# Proposal

A possible future course of action.

A proposal possesses no authority until explicitly approved.

---

# Recognition

Immediate identification of an Editorial World despite perceptual transformation.

Recognition must always remain.

---

# Technology

Any technical instrument used to implement approved perceptual objectives.

Technology possesses no creative authority.

Technology serves perception.

---

# Threshold

The architectural principle that makes Crossing possible.

Threshold is not a visual object.

Threshold is not an effect.

Threshold is the conceptual boundary through which coherent Editorial Worlds remain distinct while belonging to the same professional identity.
