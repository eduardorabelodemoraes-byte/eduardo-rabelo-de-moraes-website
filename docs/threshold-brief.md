# threshold-brief.md

# THE THRESHOLD BRIEF

## First Reading for Every Collaborator

Version 2.0

---

## Purpose

This document is the mandatory entry point to the Threshold project.

Every collaborator — human or artificial — must read and understand this document before reading any other project documentation or proposing any contribution.

Its purpose is not to explain every detail of the project.

Its purpose is to prevent fundamental misunderstandings.

---

## What Threshold Is

Threshold is an editorial digital project.

It presents the professional work of Eduardo Rabelo de Moraes through carefully designed editorial environments rather than conventional portfolio structures.

Its objective is not visual novelty.

Its objective is perceptual coherence.

Everything exists to reinforce professional judgment, precision and clarity.

---

## What Threshold Is Not

Threshold is not:

- a portfolio template;
- a corporate website;
- an agency website;
- a marketing landing page;
- a technology demonstration;
- an animation showcase;
- an artistic installation.

Any proposal based on these references is incompatible with the project.

---

## The Central Principle

Threshold is organized around editorial worlds.

Each world possesses its own identity.

The visitor does not simply navigate between pages.

The visitor gradually inhabits another coherent editorial world.

This experience is called the Crossing.

The Crossing is the architectural center of the project.

---

## The Home

The Home establishes:

- trust;
- precision;
- editorial discipline;
- professional judgment;
- calm.

Nothing should weaken these qualities.

---

## Game Localization

Game Localization is not an extension of the Home.

It is another editorial world.

It possesses different perceptual qualities while remaining faithful to the same professional identity.

---

## The Crossing

The Crossing is neither navigation nor animation.

It is the gradual perceptual transformation through which one editorial world becomes another.

The visitor should never perceive a conventional page transition.

Instead, the visitor should experience continuous spatial and perceptual continuity.

---

## Technology

Technology is never the starting point.

Never begin with:

- WebGL;
- Canvas;
- SVG;
- CSS;
- rendering techniques;
- shaders.

Begin by answering a different question:

"What should the visitor perceive?"

Only then should implementation be discussed.

---

## Experiments

Experiments exist only to answer clearly defined questions.

They do not exist to create permanent features.

Every experiment may conclude:

- Supported.
- Refuted.
- Inconclusive.

All three outcomes represent progress.

---

## Communication

Always distinguish between:

Observation.

Inference.

Proposal.

Decision.

Implementation.

Never present one category as another.

---

## If Uncertainty Appears

Stop.

Read the Manifesto.

Read the Canon.

Only then continue.

---

## Final Principle

Understand the project before attempting to improve it.

Understanding always precedes contribution.
