LEGAL BACKGROUND

A practice built through documents, procedure and consequence.

My legal experience spans more than two decades, beginning after my Bachelor of Laws at Universidade Cândido Mendes and developing through independent practice in the Brazilian legal system, particularly in civil and consumer matters.

The work required close reading of judicial filings, decisions, contracts and supporting records; legal research; structured written submissions; and the management of deadlines, evidence and confidential information.

A legal document is never only a text. It is part of a procedure, an evidentiary record and a chain of consequences.

03

LAW, DATA & TECHNOLOGY

The method now travels into new systems.

Legal operations and data-quality work depend on many of the same disciplines as legal practice: establishing what a record shows, identifying inconsistencies, applying defined criteria and documenting a conclusion that can be reviewed.

My work evaluating AI-generated language has added another layer to that method. Outputs are assessed for factual accuracy, instruction adherence, consistency and overall quality, including cases in which ambiguity requires comparative judgment rather than a mechanical answer.

I am extending this practice through advanced Microsoft Excel and ongoing study of Power BI, artificial intelligence and related digital tools. The objective is not to replace legal judgment with automation, but to make structured review more reliable, traceable and useful.
