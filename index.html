ABOUT

A practice built across law, language, technology and editorial judgment.

I am an English-to-Brazilian Portuguese translator and localization specialist, lawyer-linguist and bilingual editorial writer. My work brings together legal reasoning, linguistic precision and cultural judgment across legal translation, game and software localization, marketing transcreation, editorial content and linguistic quality.

A legal career spanning twenty-two years taught me to read for structure, evidence, confidentiality, risk and consequence. In translation and localization, that background supports decisions that allow a text to work naturally in its destination culture without losing purpose, voice or accountability.

The same attention extends to AI and digital publishing. Alongside confidential creative-content localization and transcreation workflows, I evaluate AI-generated language and publish independent bilingual Insights on language, law, technology and public-interest communication. AI, Justice, and the Rule of Law, developed by the University of Oxford’s Saïd Business School in partnership with UNESCO and completed in 2026, further deepened my focus on fairness, transparency and responsibility.

Precision without erasure.
