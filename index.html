ABOUT

A practice built across law, language and editorial work.

My work sits at the intersection of legal reasoning, linguistic precision and editorial judgment. A legal education informs how I approach structure, terminology and consequence; translation and localization bring those principles into contact with voice, audience and use.

Across legal, game, editorial and linguistic QA contexts, I translate between English and Brazilian Portuguese with particular attention to the decisions that allow a text to function naturally without losing what makes it precise.
